# References

What each source is used for in the project.

## Flocking and leadership

- **Reynolds, C. W. (1987).** Flocks, herds and schools: a distributed
  behavioral model. *Computer Graphics* 21(4), 25-34 (SIGGRAPH '87).
  The model: separation, alignment (velocity matching) and cohesion as
  accelerations, prioritized acceleration allocation, steer-to-avoid.
- **Reynolds, C. W. (1999).** Steering behaviors for autonomous characters.
  *Game Developers Conference*. Obstacle avoidance, containment, leader
  following, path following as reusable behaviours.
- **Couzin, I. D., Krause, J., Franks, N. R., Levin, S. A. (2005).**
  Effective leadership and decision-making in animal groups on the move.
  *Nature* 433, 513-516. Informed individuals guiding an uninformed group;
  the research question of this project.
- **Olfati-Saber, R. (2006).** Flocking for multi-agent dynamic systems:
  algorithms and theory. *IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control* 51(3),
  401-420. Flocking on double-integrator agents, virtual leader, obstacles.

## Simulation methodology

- **Pinciroli, C. et al. (2012).** ARGoS: a modular, parallel, multi-engine
  simulator for multi-robot systems. *Swarm Intelligence* 6(4), 271-295.
  Declarative experiment files with random robot placement (`<distribute>`);
  precedent for our map files.
  Examples: <https://www.argos-sim.info/forum/viewtopic.php?p=731>,
  <https://www.argos-sim.info/forum/viewtopic.php?p=633>
- **Fremont, D. J. et al. (2019).** Scenic: a language for scenario
  specification and scene generation. *PLDI 2019*. Scenario generation with
  a Webots interface; related work.
  <https://scenic-lang.readthedocs.io/en/3.x/modules/scenic.simulators.webots.simulator.html>
