# References

What each source is used for in the project.

## Flocking and leadership

- **Reynolds, C. W. (1987).** Flocks, herds and schools: a distributed
  behavioral model. *Computer Graphics* 21(4), 25-34 (SIGGRAPH '87).
  The model: separation, alignment (velocity matching) and cohesion as
  accelerations, prioritized acceleration allocation, steer-to-avoid.
- **Reynolds, C. W. (1999).** Steering behaviors for autonomous characters.
  *Game Developers Conference*. Obstacle avoidance, containment, leader
  following, path following as reusable behaviours.
- **Couzin, I. D., Krause, J., Franks, N. R., Levin, S. A. (2005).**
  Effective leadership and decision-making in animal groups on the move.
  *Nature* 433, 513-516. Informed individuals guiding an uninformed group;
  the research question of this project.
- **Olfati-Saber, R. (2006).** Flocking for multi-agent dynamic systems:
  algorithms and theory. *IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control* 51(3),
  401-420. Flocking on double-integrator agents, virtual leader, obstacles.

## From the ideal boid to a robot

- **Virágh, C. et al. (2014).** Flocking algorithm for autonomous flying
  robots. *Bioinspiration & Biomimetics* 9, 025012.
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.3601>. A flying robot modelled by its
  imperfections (controller relaxation time, max acceleration, delay,
  noise, refresh rate, range); delay-induced oscillations and collisions;
  linear repulsion and a pairwise viscous-friction alignment to damp them.
- **Vásárhelyi, G. et al. (2018).** Optimized flocking of autonomous drones
  in confined environments. *Science Robotics* 3(20), eaat3536. 30 real
  drones; flocking parameters tuned by evolutionary optimisation; walls
  and obstacles as virtual GPS walls. The phase-3 model.
- **robotsim**, the authors' simulator (MIT):
  <https://github.com/csviragh/robotsim>. `src/algo_spp_evol.c` (the
  model, shill agents for obstacles), `src/utilities/interactions.c`
  (repulsion, friction), `src/utilities/math_utils.c` (braking curve
  `VelDecayLinSqrt`), `src/utilities/arenas.c` (arena walls).
- **Bansal, J. C., Sethi, N., Anicho, O., Nagar, A. (2022).** Drone
  flocking optimization using
  NSGA-II and principal component analysis.
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.00432>. Re-implements Vásárhelyi's model
  with its equations written out.
- **Hauert, S. et al. (2011).** Reynolds flocking in reality with
  fixed-wing robots: communication range vs. maximum turning rate.
  *IROS 2011*, 5015-5020. Reynolds + migration point on 10 outdoor robots;
  rotorcraft approximated as omnidirectional.
- **Soria, E., Schiano, F., Floreano, D. (2019).** The influence of limited
  visual sensing on the Reynolds flocking algorithm. *IEEE IRC 2019*.
- **Soria, E., Schiano, F., Floreano, D. (2021).** Predictive control of
  aerial swarms in cluttered environments. *Nature Machine Intelligence*
  3, 545-554. Potential-field swarm models don't guarantee fast, safe
  flight in clutter; replaced by model predictive control.
- **Schilling, F. et al. (2019).** Learning vision-based flight in drone
  swarms by imitation. *IEEE RA-L*. A Reynolds-family flocking controller
  as the teacher for real quadrotors.
- **Forgoston, E., Schwartz, I. B. (2008).** Delay-induced instabilities in
  self-propelling swarms. *Physical Review E* 77, 035203.
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/0712.2950>. The delay-induced transition is a
  Hopf bifurcation.

## Crazyflie

- Bitcraze, thrust upgrade kit (stock Crazyflie 2.1: 60 g total thrust):
  <https://www.bitcraze.io/2022/10/thrust-upgrade-kit-for-the-crazyflie-2-1/>

  <https://github.com/bitcraze/crazyflie-firmware>

## Simulation methodology

- **Pinciroli, C. et al. (2012).** ARGoS: a modular, parallel, multi-engine
  simulator for multi-robot systems. *Swarm Intelligence* 6(4), 271-295.
  Declarative experiment files with random robot placement (`<distribute>`);
  precedent for our map files.
  Examples: <https://www.argos-sim.info/forum/viewtopic.php?p=731>,
  <https://www.argos-sim.info/forum/viewtopic.php?p=633>
- **Fremont, D. J. et al. (2019).** Scenic: a language for scenario
  specification and scene generation. *PLDI 2019*. Scenario generation with
  a Webots interface; related work.
  <https://scenic-lang.readthedocs.io/en/3.x/modules/scenic.simulators.webots.simulator.html>
