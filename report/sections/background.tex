\section{State of the art}
\label{sec:background}

\paragraph{Swarm robotics} designs the collective behaviour of many simple
robots acting on local sensing, without central control
\citep{brambilla2013}. In Brambilla et al.'s terms this project is
coordinated motion (flocking) carrying a collective decision (where to go)
made by a few informed members, with controllers designed by hand.

\paragraph{Flocking models.} \citet{reynolds1987} built flocks from three
local rules, separation, alignment and cohesion, each an acceleration; they
remain the usual starting point for flocking robots, and \citet{hauert2011}
flew them on fixed-wing robots. \citet{couzin2002} cast the same ingredients
as zones: an individual avoids neighbours in its zone of repulsion and,
only if there are none, aligns with and moves toward the others.
\citet{olfatisaber2006} showed that free rules break a flock with inertia
into pieces unless a navigational feedback, a virtual leader, holds it
together. \citet{viragh2014} described a flying robot by its imperfections
(response time, largest acceleration, delay, noise) and showed that delays
make ideal models oscillate and collide; \citet{vasarhelyi2018} flew 30 real
drones with a model built for this, with linear repulsion, a velocity
alignment called friction, and virtual walls, in an arena with no goal.
\citet{soria2021} showed that such reactive models do not guarantee safe
flight in clutter.

\paragraph{Leadership.} \citet{couzin2005} gave a few individuals of a
zone-model group a preferred direction, mixed into the social one with a
weight $\omega$, while the others cannot tell who they are. They found that a
small informed minority can guide a large group, more accurately the larger
its fraction; that larger groups need a smaller fraction; and that with two
informed subgroups the group follows their average direction when the two
differ little, and chooses one when they differ a lot.

\paragraph{Hybrid control.} A deliberative layer that plans on a model of the
world, on top of a reactive one that keeps the robot safe, is the classic
hybrid architecture \citep{arkin1998}; A* on a grid \citep{hart1968} and pure
pursuit \citep{coulter1992} are standard ways to plan and follow a path. This
project gives such a layer to the leaders only.
