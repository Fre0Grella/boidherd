\title{Who follows the leaders?\\
  Informed leadership in three flocking models on simulated drones}
\author{Marco Galeri\\
  \small Intelligent Robotic Systems, Alma Mater Studiorum -- Università di Bologna}
\date{November 2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
  A few leaders, which the others cannot recognise, have to bring a flock of
  simulated Crazyflie drones to a goal only they know. Three flocking models,
  Reynolds', Couzin's and a hybrid built on Vásárhelyi's model for real
  drones, fly on the same robot behind one interface, and the leaders can
  plan a path on the known map. In 450 runs on maps no model was tuned on,
  the planner is what gets Reynolds' flock past a corner or a dead end; more
  leaders help, as Couzin predicted, only in Couzin's model; and the hybrid
  fails with two leaders that go round an obstacle on different sides, since
  its flock follows their average. What a model makes a follower do explains
  each one's strengths and failures.
\end{abstract}
